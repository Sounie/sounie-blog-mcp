EmbeddingGemma 2 (q8 ONNX)

This distribution bundles the files embeddinggemma-2-onnx/model_quantized.onnx and
embeddinggemma-2-onnx/model_quantized.onnx_data from onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX at revision
daa72c51243991dfcaf9f9137d2c573d8f7790c0
(https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX).

They are a modified version of google/embeddinggemma-2 at revision
914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b (https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2): the model
was converted to ONNX and its weights quantised to 8 bits by onnx-community. The files are bundled
unchanged from that conversion.
