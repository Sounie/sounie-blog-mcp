# Clarifying EmbeddingGemma 2 tokenizer redistribution terms

**Bottom line:** Google’s version-specific documentation identifies EmbeddingGemma 2 as Apache 2.0-licensed, and the official Hugging Face repository metadata at revision `914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b` agrees. That is stronger, more precise evidence than the general Gemma Terms of Use, whose Appendix lists “EmbeddingGemma” without a version number but does not name “EmbeddingGemma 2.” The evidence therefore supports redistributing this repository’s `tokenizer.json` under Apache 2.0, including in an application, subject to Apache’s conditions. The exact tokenizer is **32,170,510 bytes** (about 32.17 MB decimal; 30.68 MiB), but the reviewed sources do not give it a separate, file-level license grant. The Terms’ scope ambiguity remains unresolved by the official sources, so this is a source-based interpretation, not legal advice.

## Google identifies EmbeddingGemma 2 as Apache 2.0

**Verified facts:** Google’s EmbeddingGemma documentation says that “EmbeddingGemma 2 is provided with open weights and licensed under the Apache 2.0 License.” Google’s launch announcement likewise describes it as released under a “commercially permissive Apache 2.0 license.” The official model card at the requested Hugging Face revision declares `license: apache-2.0` and links to Google’s Apache license page. These are model-specific sources, not an inference from unrelated repository metadata. ([Google EmbeddingGemma documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/embeddinggemma); [Google launch announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/); [pinned official model card](https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2/blob/914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b/README.md))

The exact-revision Hugging Face API reports the repository as `google/embeddinggemma-2`, identifies its revision as `914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b`, and reports `cardData.license: apache-2.0`. Its pinned file listing includes `tokenizer.json`, sized **32,170,510 bytes**, with SHA-256 `4d777ef5bdc1aa36227abdfb77c3e49e7b9c892d16e1b6bda41c393504828be4`. The listing does not show a standalone `LICENSE` or `NOTICE` file, and it does not attach a separate license field to `tokenizer.json`. ([pinned model API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/google/embeddinggemma-2/revision/914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b); [pinned repository tree](https://huggingface.co/api/models/google/embeddinggemma-2/tree/914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b?recursive=true&expand=true))

**Interpretation:** The best-supported classification for this v2 release is Apache 2.0—not the additional redistribution regime in the general Gemma Terms. But the license evidence is stated at the model/repository level. It reasonably supports treating repository files, including the tokenizer, as part of the Apache-licensed release; it is not an express, standalone grant for the JSON file.

## The general Gemma Terms leave a naming ambiguity

Google’s Gemma Terms say they apply to Gemma models listed in their Appendix and define “Gemma” as models, trained weights, and parameters “identified in the Appendix.” That Appendix includes “EmbeddingGemma,” without a version number, but does not separately name “EmbeddingGemma 2”; the page also directs readers to separate Gemma 4 terms. ([Google Gemma Terms of Use](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms))

**Interpretation and limit:** The distinct, version-specific Apache statements for EmbeddingGemma 2 are the clearest evidence for this pinned release. However, the official materials do not say explicitly that EmbeddingGemma 2 is excluded from the unversioned Appendix entry, nor explain whether that entry covers later versions. They also do not expressly decide whether the tokenizer alone is independently licensed as an Apache “Work.” Thus, the conclusion that the old Gemma Terms do not govern this exact tokenizer is well-supported but not unambiguous on the reviewed record.

## Apache permits redistribution with license and notice conditions

Google’s hosted Apache 2.0 text expressly permits reproduction and distribution “in any medium, with or without modifications,” subject to its conditions. In particular, §4(a) requires giving recipients a copy of the license; §4(b) requires prominent notices on modified files stating that they were changed; §4(c) requires retaining applicable copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution notices from source form in distributed source derivatives; and §4(d) requires passing along readable attribution notices if the Work includes a `NOTICE` file. ([Google-hosted Apache License 2.0, §§4(a)–(d)](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/apache_2))

**Practical reading:** If redistributing the tokenizer as part of the EmbeddingGemma 2 release, include a copy of Apache 2.0, preserve any applicable notices actually supplied with the source, and visibly identify any modifications to the file. Apache §4(d)’s `NOTICE` carry-through is conditional on the Work including such a file; the pinned repository tree does not list one. That snapshot does not establish that no notice exists in any other upstream distribution. The quoted Apache conditions do not themselves impose a general marketing or product-UI credit to Google, although this report cannot rule out obligations arising from material outside the reviewed snapshot.

The general Gemma Terms prescribe a different regime **if they apply**: §3.1 requires a copy of those Terms for recipients, flow-down of use restrictions, prominent notices on modified files, and a `Notice` file with the text “Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms”; §3.2 incorporates the Prohibited Use Policy and prohibits unlawful use. Those are not Apache §4 requirements. Because the sources do not conclusively resolve whether the older, unversioned “EmbeddingGemma” entry reaches version 2, do not silently treat those additional conditions as settled for this artifact. ([Google Gemma Terms of Use, §§3.1–3.2](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms); [Google Gemma Prohibited Use Policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy))

## Conclusion

The official-source record supports bundling the pinned 32,170,510-byte tokenizer in an application under the EmbeddingGemma 2 Apache 2.0 designation, with an Apache license copy and any applicable upstream notices preserved; mark changes if the file is modified. The unresolved points are the breadth of the Gemma Terms’ unversioned “EmbeddingGemma” Appendix entry and whether Google intended a distinct file-level license for `tokenizer.json`. For a distribution decision requiring certainty on either point, seek legal advice or written clarification from Google rather than treating this interpretation as a definitive legal determination.
